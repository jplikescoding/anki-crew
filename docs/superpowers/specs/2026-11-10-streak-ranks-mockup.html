Our own rank crests

League-spirited, but original. Each rank keeps everything the last had and adds a piece: Bronze is a shield with laurels. Silver grows side blades. Gold gets wings and a crown. Platinum's wings widen around a teal gem with a halo. Diamond turns the shield into cut crystal.
